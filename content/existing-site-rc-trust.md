## Mission & Description
The group operates at the intersection of computer science and law. They employ "a human-centred computing approach" to examine how technology can align with legal requirements and digital rights. Work addresses "governance, audit, rights, security, privacy, safety and trust regarding emerging and data-driven technologies" through interdisciplinary and socio-technical frameworks.
