- Full roster with photos, bios, links (sub-page `/team`)

## Design Directions

### Aesthetic
- Brutalist / modern — stark white/black palette, single violet accent (`#8b1a4a`)
- No serif fonts — Geist Sans throughout
- Editorial layout: ruled lines, mono labels, divider-separated lists
- Key visual: animated canvas grid (black bg), not full-screen height

### Principles
- Academic, not product — restrained typography (`font-semibold`, not `font-black`)
- Content-first: the work and people should read clearly
- Sub-pages for everything with depth; homepage stays light

### References
- Agency/editorial brutalism
- Knowing Machines (knowingmachines.org)
- Academic team roster sites
