## Deadline

Site should be **live at least one week before CHI**. CHI 2026 runs late April / early May 2026, so target launch: **~mid-April 2026**.

## Required Sections

1. **Overview / About** — group description, mission, research themes
2. **Team Members** — with roles, bios, links
3. **Projects** — funded research initiatives
4. **Publications** — ideally sourced from BibTeX; must be easily updateable
5. **Latest News** — recent updates, events, announcements; must be easily updateable
6. **Awards & Achievements** — e.g. best paper awards (can link to/overlap with news)

## Design Direction

- **Aesthetic**: retro / cyberpunk / synthwave — neon purple, orange, and similar as **accent/highlight colours**, not the dominant scheme
- **Overall tone**: tasteful, restrained — academic credibility first, aesthetic as flavour
- Not flashy; purposeful
- "Boring but tasteful" is the explicit brief from the PI

## Key Requirements

- **Publications updatable via BibTeX** (strongly preferred)
- **News / content editable without developer involvement** — CMS considered (WordPress mentioned); decision pending
- Site must be straightforward to maintain by non-developers on the team
